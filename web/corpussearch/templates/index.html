Check whether a piece of text — or a substantial part of it — appears in a large document corpus, using document fingerprinting.

How it works

  1. Pick a corpus to check against.
  2. Add your text — type or paste it, load a premade example, or add the corpus-specific generic filler.
  3. Choose one or more detection algorithms and run the check.
  4. Optional: once you have results, highlight the matching passages to see exactly which parts overlap.

We extract a compact set of fingerprints from your text and look for the same fingerprints in the corpus.

Required for corpus search, detection, and highlighting.

    How highlighting works

    Highlights cover word groups in your text whose fingerprints are stored for the selected document. The index keeps only some fingerprints, so matching words can remain unhighlighted.

    • Largest aligned passage: the chain with the most distinct fingerprints, not necessarily the most characters.
    • All aligned passages: all chains that meet the clustering settings, including separate passages.
    • All shared fingerprints: matching word groups anywhere in the document, even if they do not form a chain.
